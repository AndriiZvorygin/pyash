# Roadmap Update Rules

- Use today’s date to decide which completed section to extend.
- When a tranche is done, remove its “Week” block and record completion in the timeline summary.
- Keep only upcoming work in Week blocks; completed work lives in dated timeline sections.
- Keep a `# TODO` header immediately before the first upcoming Week block.
- Week blocks are sequential and contiguous: Week N starts the day after the previous Week ends.
- When shifting Week blocks, update their date ranges and headings together.
