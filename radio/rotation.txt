# The order the station plays.
#
# The songs that came before the radio go first, so the programme opens with
# what the house already made. After them come the lanes of the rotation, in
# the order of radio/topics.txt.
#
# A name here is a song the station is allowed to play even when it is not a
# lane of the rotation. A name here is also a song the station plays first.
#
# A song the house has no words for stays out of the programme: a listener
# reads along with the song, and a song without words has nothing to read.
# The audio of those songs stays in the catalogue folder and in the import
# manifest, and comes back if the house ever writes words for it.
