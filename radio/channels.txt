# The channels of the station.
#
# A radio that plays one programme at one pace is a radio that never rests.
# Every channel is a name, the voice that sings it, the tempo it is rendered
# at, the style it is rendered in, and the lanes it carries:
#
#   <name> | <engine> | <bpm> | <style> | <lane>, <lane>, ...
#
# The name is what a listener asks for: the stream of a channel is
# /<name>.mp3, a plain mp3 that any player can open. The engine is the voice:
# the song of a lane in a channel is named for it, so the night channel sings
# night-peace.mp3 and carries the words of the peace lane.
#
# The lane list may be "*", which is every lane of radio/topics.txt. A style of
# "-" is the style the workflow carries of its own.
#
# The first channel of the file is the default one: its stream is
# /stream.mp3, and the songs that came before the radio play in it.

# The day programme: the whole rotation, at the tempo the house has always
# sung at.
day | ace-step | 128 | - | *

# The night programme: slow, calm, and sung like a hymn. The style is the one
# the house already sang God Remembers All in, and the tempo is the slow one.
# The lanes are the calm ones, and the two hymns the house already sang slow.
night | night | 75 | plain Mennonite hymn, natural male lead, soft female response, slow acoustic hymn, call and response, clear dry vocals, 75 bpm | acceptance, contemplation, god-remembers, gratitude, harmony, holy-spirit, patience, peace, stillness, solitude
